## Personal backgrounds

Use a custom image or Capy pixel pattern behind conversations and the new-thread page. Six saved slots let you switch between backgrounds. Apply a background to all threads or keep it on the new-thread page.

## This fork

Based on Mateo Cerquetella’s Aura, this edition adds warped blue-noise dithering, an animated half-wallpaper fade, image caching improvements, and readable composer and recent-thread surfaces on desktop and compact screens. Original licensing and third-party notices are included.

## Requirements

Requires BB with content-script support. No external account or paid service is required. Background images and settings remain on your BB installation.

[Source and installation](https://github.com/jshph/bb-extensions/tree/main/plugins/aura)
