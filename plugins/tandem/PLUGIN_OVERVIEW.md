Talk with Codex or Claude in your BB thread using Codex subscription voice. Tandem keeps the conversation connected as you navigate.

## What you get

Start from the waveform beside dictation, including on New thread. Mute and circular hang-up controls stay visible during a call; a floating dial follows you off-thread. The waveform pulses when connected and glows during assistant playback. End voice while coding work continues, and use BB’s thread controls to cancel agent work. Tool approvals remain in BB.

Optionally enable Cached briefing to supply a bounded prepared reference when a call starts. Missing or stale briefings leave the call retrieval-only. Briefings use your Codex subscription and remain local to your BB installation.

## Requirements

Tandem is an experimental developer preview. It uses Codex signed in with ChatGPT on the BB server, with no API-key fallback. Claude retains its existing provider authentication. Native Codex voice requires the customized BB native transport bridge; new-thread startup requires input-free initialization support. General stock-BB compatibility is not established. Codex protocol changes may require updates. Physical-device audio routing and background behavior need further testing.

The display name is Tandem; the stable plugin ID remains live-voice to preserve settings and saved data.
